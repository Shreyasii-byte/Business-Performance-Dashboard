# 📊 Business Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive Business Performance Dashboard built using Power BI to monitor key business metrics, evaluate market performance, and support data-driven decision making.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to track performance trends, analyze product-level insights, and identify business growth opportunities through interactive visualizations.

---

## Project Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance using KPIs
- Track trends and business growth over time
- Analyze product and market performance
- Support evidence-based decision making
- Identify opportunities for optimization and growth

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance through:

- KPI Cards
- Performance Metrics
- Trend Analysis
- Revenue & Growth Tracking
- Interactive Filters

### 2. Product & Market Analysis

Provides deeper insights into:

- Product Performance
- Market Analysis
- Category Comparisons
- Customer Trends
- Growth Opportunities
